Data quality

One person, one single record.

When the same doctor appears twice, their visits, sales and commissions are split across two records and nobody sees the real picture. delcos finds duplicated records even when they aren’t spelt the same, and helps you merge them without losing anything.

  1. Warns before it happens

    When you create a record, it compares the name with the records you can see, even with missing accents, a different word order or a different letter. If it looks like another one, it shows you the candidates with their centre, their town and the percentage of similarity.

  2. Watches your whole portfolio

    It looks for pairs that are 80 % similar or more. The ones you mark as different people never come back.

  3. Compare side by side

    You choose which record stays and, field by field, which name, phone, email or stage to keep. Missing custom fields are filled in from the other one.

  4. Merge without losing anything

    Visits, appointments, practices, sales, clients, calls and accounts move to the record that remains. The other one is archived, not deleted, and the merge is logged.

And when you import from Excel, each client is recognised by their tax ID, email or name, and each product by its reference, barcode or name: you decide whether to update it or leave it as it is. Orders that already exist, by their number, are not duplicated.

The channel

A direct channel with those who care.

Your professionals log in to delcos with their own access, which their rep grants from their record. What today gets lost between emails, messages and calls moves into a single two-way channel, linked to their record.

  • AvailableTheir report, always up to date

    What has been sold on their recommendation month by month, their position among professionals and how consistent their patients are: how many started, repeat, stopped after the first order or have not ordered for over 60 days. Figures only, and from 5 patients up.

  • AvailableInstant alerts

    Every time an order is logged in their name, they get an alert.

  • AvailableMaterials and questions

    They request the materials your company offers and send you their questions. You get an alert and it stays in their record; they see when you deliver it and your reply.

  • AvailableCentre orders

    The centre restocks only the products it has set up, at its own price. It reaches you as a draft to approve, and the centre sees whether it is pending, approved or packed.

Their report and alerts come with the Company and Custom plans. Patient consistency, materials, messages and centre orders are an optional module.
